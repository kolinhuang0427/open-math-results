# Fioravanti–Kompatscher–Rossi (`arXiv:2602.04034v1`): Conjecture 59 (`conjecture:strong`) & Conjecture 1 (`conjecture:main`)

## Exact Statement (verbatim from `tools/.arxiv_cache/eprint/2602.04034v1/main.tex`)

### Section 7 (*Conclusion*), Conjecture 59 (`\label{conjecture:strong}`, lines 1511–1515):
> \begin{conjecture} \label{conjecture:strong}
> Let $\mathbf{A}$ be a finite abelian $p$-group, and let $\mathbf{R}$ be a ring (considered as regular $\mathbf{R}$-module), such that $p$ is invertible in $\mathbf{R}$. Then, there is a $k\in \mathbb{N}$, such that $\mathcal O_{\mathbf{A},\mathbf{R}}$ is uniformly generated by $k$-ary $(\mathbf{A},\mathbf{R})$-minors.
> \end{conjecture}
>
> Note that, by Proposition \ref{proposition:ugproduct}, Conjecture \ref{conjecture:strong} implies the same statement holds for all abelian groups $\mathbf{A}$ such that $|A|$ is invertible in $\mathbf{R}$. Any module has clearly an abelian group as a reduct, thus we moreover get the same statement for modules $\mathbf{A}$. Last, by Corollary \ref{corollary:orbitsupport}, Conjecture \ref{conjecture:strong} further implies that $\clodO_{\mathbf{A},\mathbf{B}}$ is uniformly generated by $k$-ary $(\mathbf{A},\mathbf{B})$-minors for some $k$, for \emph{every} $\mathbf{R}$-module $\mathbf{B}$. In particular, this means that Conjecture \ref{conjecture:strong} implies Conjecture \ref{conjecture:main}.

### Section 1 (*Introduction*), Conjecture 1 (`\label{conjecture:main}`, lines 221–223):
> \begin{conjecture} \label{conjecture:main}
> Let $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ be finite modules. Then, there are only finitely many clonoids from $\mathbf{A}$ to $\mathbf{B}$, if and only if $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ are of coprime order.
> \end{conjecture}
