# Saikia (2026), arXiv:2611.28840v1 — Conjecture 8.1 (`\label{conj:sharp}`)

## Primary Source & Verbatim Statement

From Manjil P. Saikia, *"Congruences modulo powers of 2 and 3 for Lin's function $\mathrm{PDO}_t(n)$"*, `arXiv:2611.28840v1` (21 Nov 2026), Section 8 (`\section{Concluding remarks}\label{sec:final}`), `final.tex` lines 931–941 (quoted verbatim):

> Arguing as in the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:internal}, we conclude that for $3\leq k\leq11$, there exists an odd integer $\lambda_k$ such that, for any $n\geq0$,
> \begin{equation}\label{eq:sharp}
> \PDO\bigl(3\times2^{k+2}n\bigr)\equiv8\lambda_k\,\PDO\bigl(3\times2^{k}n\bigr)\pmod{2^{\lfloor7(k+1)/2\rfloor}},
> \end{equation}
> and that the modulus in \eqref{eq:sharp} is best possible for these values of $k$.
>
> This motivates us to make the following conjecture.
> \begin{conjecture}\label{conj:sharp}
> For any $k\geq3$, there exists an odd integer $\lambda_k$ such that \eqref{eq:sharp} holds for any $n\geq0$.
> \end{conjecture}

Here `\PDO` is defined in `final.tex` line 39 as $\mathrm{PDO}_{\mathrm{t}}(n)$ (the total number of tagged parts over all partitions of $n$ into odd parts with designated summands, where the subscript $\mathrm{t}$ stands for "tagged", not a parameter), with generating function `eq:gf` $\sum_{n \ge 0} \mathrm{PDO}_{\mathrm{t}}(n) q^n = q f_2 f_3^2 f_{12}^2 / (f_1^2 f_6)$, and `eq:gfm` reduces $n \mapsto \mathrm{PDO}_{\mathrm{t}}(3 \times 2^{m+2} n)$ with $k = m+2$ to the sequence of vectors $\mathbf{x}_m$ generated by $\mathbf{x}_{m+1} = \mathbf{x}_m A$.
