# Navarra, Qureshi & Rinaldo (2025), Conjecture 2.3 (`conj`)

## Statement

Primary source: `arXiv:2511.22346v1`, Section 2 ("Switching rook polynomial of a collection of cells and a computational approach", `HILBERT_Series_NEW.tex`, lines 645–647), Conjecture 2.3 (`\label{conj}`):

> Let $\mathcal{P}$ be a collection of cells. Then the switching rook polynomial of $\mathcal{P}$ coincides with the $h$-polynomial of $K[\mathcal{P}]$, and the rook number of $\mathcal{P}$ equals the regularity of $K[\mathcal{P}]$.

Context (`HILBERT_Series_NEW.tex`, Sections 1 and 2):
- A collection of cells $\mathcal{P}$ is a finite set of unit squares $[a, a+(1,1)]$ in $\mathbb{N}^2$, with vertex set $V(\mathcal{P})$, $S_{\mathcal{P}} = K[x_v : v \in V(\mathcal{P})]$ over a field $K$, and inner $2$-minor ideal $I_{\mathcal{P}} \subset S_{\mathcal{P}}$ generated by $x_a x_b - x_c x_d$ over all inner intervals $[a, b] \subseteq \mathcal{P}$ (where $a,b$ are diagonal corners and $c,d$ are anti-diagonal corners). $K[\mathcal{P}] = S_{\mathcal{P}} / I_{\mathcal{P}}$.
- Two rooks in cells $C_1, C_2 \in \mathcal{P}$ are non-attacking iff they do not lie in a common row or column (maximal horizontal/vertical cell interval) of $\mathcal{P}$; they are in switching position iff they occupy diagonal (resp. anti-diagonal) corner cells of an inner interval of $\mathcal{P}$. $\tilde{r}_{\mathcal{P}}(t) = \sum_{j=0}^{r(\mathcal{P})} \tilde{r}_j(\mathcal{P}) t^j$ counts $j$-rook configurations modulo switching equivalence, and $r(\mathcal{P})$ is the rook number.
- Verified computationally in Theorem 2.2 for all collections of cells of rank $|\mathcal{P}| \le 10$ and all polyominoes of rank $|\mathcal{P}| \le 12$.
