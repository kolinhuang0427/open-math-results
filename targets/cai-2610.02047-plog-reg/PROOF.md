# Proof: FPT-Sized Regular Resolution Refutations Parameterized by Partially Log-Weighted Incidence Treewidth

## Answer and Main Theorem

**Answer:** **Yes.** Every unsatisfiable CNF formula $F$ admits an FPT-sized regular resolution refutation parameterized by its partially log-weighted incidence treewidth $\mathrm{tw}_{\mathrm{plog}}^*(F)$.

**Theorem 1.** Let $F$ be an unsatisfiable CNF formula (without empty or tautological clauses, per Section 3 of `arXiv:2610.02047v1`) with $n$ variables, $m$ clauses, maximum clause width $k \ge 1$, and partially log-weighted incidence treewidth $w := \mathrm{tw}_{\mathrm{plog}}^*(F)$. Then $F$ has a **regular resolution refutation** of length
$$|\pi'| \le k^2(n+m)\, 2^{O(w \log(w+2))} \le (n+m)^3\, 2^{O(w \log(w+2))},$$
which is FPT-sized parameterized by $\mathrm{tw}_{\mathrm{plog}}^*(F)$ (since $k \le n$ and $w \ge \mathrm{tw}^*(F) \ge 1$).

*(Note: By Theorem `thm:plog-one-sided` of `arXiv:2610.02047v1`, $\mathrm{tw}_{\mathrm{plog}}^*(F) \le \mathrm{tw}_{\mathrm{os}}(F)$, so Theorem 1 also recovers FPT-sized regular resolution parameterized by one-sided incidence treewidth up to the same FPT regime, while strictly generalizing Theorem `thm:regular-main-bounds` from $\mathrm{tw}_{\log}^*(F)$ to $\mathrm{tw}_{\mathrm{plog}}^*(F)$.)*

## 1. Preliminaries and Setup from `arXiv:2610.02047v1`

Let $F$ be an unsatisfiable CNF formula with $n$ variables, $m$ clauses, and maximum clause width $k \ge 1$. Since $F$ is unsatisfiable and has no empty clauses, $G^*(F)$ contains at least one edge, so $w = \mathrm{tw}_{\mathrm{plog}}^*(F) \ge \mathrm{tw}^*(F) \ge 1$, and since $F$ has no tautological clauses, $k \le n$.

By Lemma `lem:plog-decomposition-size` of `arXiv:2610.02047v1`, there exist a nice tree decomposition $(T,\chi,r)$ of $G^*(F)$ and a clause-path family $\mathcal P = (P_c)_{c\in F}$ such that
$$w_{\mathrm{plog}}(T,\chi,r,\mathcal P) \le w \qquad\text{and}\qquad |V(T)| = O(k(n+m)w).$$
Recall the standard notation from `arXiv:2610.02047v1`:
- For each $t \in V(T)$, $T_t$ is the subtree of $T$ rooted at $t$, $\mathrm{Var}_t := \bigcup_{u \in V(T_t)} \chi_v(u)$, and $\mathrm{Cls}_t := \bigcup_{u \in V(T_t)} \chi_c(u)$.
- For $c \in \chi_c(t)$, $c_t^{\mathrm{int}} := \bigvee_{\ell \in c,\ \mathrm{var}(\ell)\in \mathrm{Var}_t} \ell$ and $c_t^{\mathrm{ext}} := \bigvee_{\ell \in c,\ \mathrm{var}(\ell)\notin \mathrm{Var}_t} \ell$.
- A state $(t,\alpha,A)$ consists of $t \in V(T)$, $\alpha:\chi_v(t)\to\{0,1\}$, and $A \subseteq \chi_c(t)$. Let $N(t,\alpha,A)$ be the set of assignments $\beta:\mathrm{Var}_t\to\{0,1\}$ extending $\alpha$ that satisfy all clauses in $(\mathrm{Cls}_t \setminus \chi_c(t)) \cup A$. The state $(t,\alpha,A)$ is *inconsistent* if $N(t,\alpha,A) = \emptyset$.
- **Upward monotonicity:** If $A \subseteq A' \subseteq \chi_c(t)$ and $(t,\alpha,A)$ is inconsistent, then $(t,\alpha,A')$ is also inconsistent.
- **Satisfied-clause equivalence:** Let $U_\alpha(S) := \{c \in S : \alpha \text{ does not satisfy } c\}$. For any $S \subseteq \chi_c(t)$, $(t,\alpha,S)$ is inconsistent if and only if $(t,\alpha,U_\alpha(S))$ is inconsistent.
- $C_\alpha := \bigvee_{x\in\chi_v(t)} (\neg x \text{ if } \alpha(x)=1 \text{ else } x)$ is the multiclause of literals falsified by $\alpha$.
- For $c \in F$, $P_c$ is either $\emptyset$ or a root-to-leaf path in $T(c) := \{t\in V(T) : c \in \chi_c(t)\}$. Define
  $$P_t := \{c \in \chi_c(t) : t \in P_c\}, \qquad B_t(A) := A \cap P_t.$$
  For $c \in \chi_c(t)$, $\omega_{\mathcal P}(t,c) = 1$ if $t \in P_c$, and $\omega_{\mathcal P}(t,c) = \max\{\log_2(\rho_{\mathcal P}(c)+1), 1\}$ otherwise, where $\rho_{\mathcal P}(c) = |\{\ell \in c : \mathrm{var}(\ell) \notin \bigcup_{u \in P_c}\chi_v(u)\}|$.

**Lemma 1 (Off-Path Internal Width Bound).** For every $t \in V(T)$ and $c \in \chi_c(t) \setminus P_t$:
1. $P_c \cap V(T_t) = \emptyset$, and hence $\mathrm{var}(c_t^{\mathrm{int}}) \setminus \chi_v(t)$ is disjoint from $\bigcup_{u\in P_c} \chi_v(u)$.
2. $|c_t^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t)| \le \rho_{\mathcal P}(c) \le 2^{\omega_{\mathcal P}(t,c)} - 1$, and $|c_t^{\mathrm{int}}| \le |\chi_v(t)| + \rho_{\mathcal P}(c) \le (w+2)(\rho_{\mathcal P}(c)+1)$.
3. Consequently,
   $$\prod_{c \in \chi_c(t) \setminus P_t} (\rho_{\mathcal P}(c)+1) \le 2^{\sum_{c\in \chi_c(t)\setminus P_t} \omega_{\mathcal P}(t,c)} \le 2^{w+1},$$
   and since $|\chi(t)| \le |\chi_v(t)| + \sum_{c\in\chi_c(t)} \omega_{\mathcal P}(t,c) \le w+1$,
   $$\prod_{c \in \chi_c(t) \setminus P_t} |c_t^{\mathrm{int}}| \le (w+2)^{|\chi_c(t)|} \cdot 2^{w+1} \le (w+2)^{w+1} 2^{w+1} = 2^{O(w\log(w+2))}.$$

*Proof.* Since $c \in \chi_c(t)$, we have $t \in T(c)$. Because $T(c)$ is a connected subtree of the rooted tree $T$, its root $r_c$ is an ancestor of $t$. For any $u \in V(T_t) \cap T(c)$, the unique path in $T(c)$ from $r_c$ to $u$ passes through $t$. Since $P_c$ is either empty or a path in $T(c)$ starting at $r_c$, $t \notin P_c$ forces $P_c \cap V(T_t) = \emptyset$. Every variable $x \in \mathrm{Var}_t \setminus \chi_v(t)$ appears only in bags strictly inside $T_t \setminus \{t\}$, so $x \notin \bigcup_{u \in P_c}\chi_v(u)$. Since $c$ is non-tautological, distinct literals of $c_t^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t)$ have distinct variables outside $\bigcup_{u\in P_c}\chi_v(u)$, giving $|c_t^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t)| \le \rho_{\mathcal P}(c)$. By definition of $\omega_{\mathcal P}(t,c)$, $\log_2(\rho_{\mathcal P}(c)+1) \le \omega_{\mathcal P}(t,c)$, and $|\chi_v(t)| + \sum_{c\in\chi_c(t)} \omega_{\mathcal P}(t,c) \le w+1$. Items (2) and (3) follow immediately (noting $|\chi_v(t)| + \rho_{\mathcal P}(c) \le w + 1 + \rho_{\mathcal P}(c) \le (w+2)(\rho_{\mathcal P}(c)+1)$). $\square$

## 2. Post-Order Variable Ordering and Hybrid Multiclause Expansion $\mathcal E_{\mathrm{plog}}$

Fix a depth-first post-order traversal of the rooted tree $(T,\chi,r)$, visiting the two children $t_1, t_2$ of each join node $t_0$ in the order $t_1$ then $t_2$. Because $\chi(r) = \emptyset$ and $\{t \in V(T) : x \in \chi_v(t)\}$ is a connected subtree of $T$, every variable $x \in \mathrm{var}(F)$ is forgotten at a unique forget-variable node $\mathrm{forg}(x) \in V(T)$. Order $\mathrm{var}(F)$ by increasing post-order index of $\mathrm{forg}(x)$, and write every clause or subclause $c'$ in this induced variable order: $c' = \ell_1 \lor \dots \lor \ell_r$ with $\mathrm{forg}(\mathrm{var}(\ell_1)) < \dots < \mathrm{forg}(\mathrm{var}(\ell_r))$.

**Observation 1 (Post-Order Separation).**
1. At any node $t \in V(T)$, every $y \in \mathrm{Var}_t \setminus \chi_v(t)$ precedes every $x \in \chi_v(t)$: $\mathrm{forg}(y) < \mathrm{forg}(x)$.
2. At any join node $t_0$ with ordered children $t_1, t_2$, every $y_1 \in \mathrm{Var}_{t_1} \setminus \chi_v(t_0)$ precedes every $y_2 \in \mathrm{Var}_{t_2} \setminus \chi_v(t_0)$, which precedes every $x \in \chi_v(t_0)$:
   $$y_1 < y_2 < x.$$

For any nonempty ordered clause $c' = \ell_1 \lor \dots \lor \ell_r$ ($r \ge 1$), define
$$\mathcal C_1(c') := \bigl\{ \neg \ell_i \uplus \ell_{i+1} \uplus \dots \uplus \ell_r : 1 \le i \le r \bigr\}, \qquad \mathcal C_0(c') := \{c'\},$$
and for the empty clause $\bot$ set $\mathcal C_1(\bot) := \emptyset$ and $\mathcal C_0(\bot) := \{\bot\}$.

For each inconsistent state $(t,\alpha,A)$, define the hybrid target multiclause family $\mathcal E_{\mathrm{plog}}(t,\alpha,A)$ as follows: if $c_t^{\mathrm{int}} = \bot$ for some $c \in A \setminus P_t$, set $\mathcal E_{\mathrm{plog}}(t,\alpha,A) := \emptyset$; otherwise, $\mathcal E_{\mathrm{plog}}(t,\alpha,A)$ consists of all multiclauses (after reducing duplicate literals via contraction so every literal has multiplicity at most $1$) of the form
$$M(t,\alpha,A,\vec C) := C_\alpha \uplus \biguplus_{c \in B_t(A) \cap U_\alpha(A)} c_t^{\mathrm{ext}} \uplus \biguplus_{c \in A \setminus P_t} C_c \uplus \biguplus_{c \in (\chi_c(t) \setminus P_t \setminus A) \cap U_\alpha(\chi_c(t))} c_t^{\mathrm{int}},$$
where $\vec C = (C_c)_{c\in A\setminus P_t} \in \prod_{c\in A\setminus P_t} \mathcal C_1(c_t^{\mathrm{int}})$.

*(Note on multiplicity: In $\mathrm{mRes}$, contractions are absorbed into each derivation step at zero length cost; here we represent each $M \in \mathcal E_{\mathrm{plog}}(t,\alpha,A)$ in reduced multiclause form where every literal present has multiplicity $1$, i.e., $M$ is identified with $\operatorname{red}(M)$ as a multiset without repetitions.)*

At the root $r$, $\chi(r) = \emptyset$, so $M(r,\emptyset,\emptyset,\emptyset) = \bot$.

## 3. Inductive Invariants for Regularity and Auxiliary Lemma

We maintain a single $\mathrm{mRes}$ derivation $\pi$ (with proof DAG $G_\pi$), constructed bottom-up along $T$. Let $\pi'$ be its image under the map of Lemma `lem:mres-to-res` (deleting tautological multiclauses and replacing each non-variable-eliminating resolution step on $v$ where $v \in \operatorname{red}(R)$ or $\neg v \in \operatorname{red}(R)$ by weakening/repetition from the single premise containing that surviving literal). For every non-tautological vertex $M$ in $G_\pi$, write $\mathrm{ResVars}(M)$ for the set of variables resolved in variable-eliminating resolution steps along all directed paths ending at $M$ in $\pi'$ (equivalently, in $G_\pi$ after dropping edges from tautological or unused premises as in Lemma `lem:mres-to-res` lines 4376–4384).

We prove that for every node $t \in V(T)$, every inconsistent state $(t,\alpha,A)$, and every choice tuple $\vec C \in \prod_{c\in A\setminus P_t} \mathcal C_1(c_t^{\mathrm{int}})$, the multiclause $M = M(t,\alpha,A,\vec C)$ can be derived in $\mathrm{mRes}$ satisfying:
- **(P0) Exactness:** The derived multiclause equals $M(t,\alpha,A,\vec C)$ (each literal in it has multiplicity $1$).
- **(P1) Locality of resolved variables:** If $M$ is non-tautological, then $\mathrm{ResVars}(M) \subseteq \mathrm{Var}_t \setminus \chi_v(t)$.
- **(P2) Path regularity:** Every directed path ending at $M$ in $\pi'$ resolves each variable at most once.
- **(P3) Active-variable purity:** If $M$ is non-tautological, then $\mathrm{var}(M) \cap \mathrm{ResVars}(M) = \emptyset$.

*(Remark on tautological targets: Whenever a target multiclause $T$ is tautological, containing both $v$ and $\neg v$ for some variable $v$, we derive $T$ directly by weakening the axiom $v \uplus \neg v$. This takes $1$ step, adds zero vertices to $\pi'$, and trivially satisfies (P0)–(P3).)*

**Lemma 2 (Safe Sequential Elimination).** Let $G$ be a non-tautological multiclause (set of literals), let $E$ be a finite multiclause, and let $H = \ell_1 \lor \dots \lor \ell_q$ ($q \ge 1$) be an ordered non-tautological clause of distinct variables such that $\mathrm{var}(E) \cap \mathrm{var}(H) = \emptyset$. Suppose:
- We have a derivation of $L_0 = G \uplus E \uplus \ell_1 \uplus \dots \uplus \ell_q$ (satisfying (P2) and (P3)), or an axiom/initial clause weakened to $L_0$; and
- For each $j \in \{1,\dots,q\}$, we have a derivation of $R_j = G \uplus \neg \ell_j \uplus \ell_{j+1} \uplus \dots \uplus \ell_q \uplus T_j$ (where every literal of $T_j$ already belongs to $G$, so $\operatorname{red}(R_j) = G \cup \{\neg\ell_j,\ell_{j+1},\dots,\ell_q\}$) satisfying (P2) and (P3), and such that $\mathrm{ResVars}(R_j)$ is disjoint from $\mathrm{var}(E)$ (which holds automatically whenever $\mathrm{var}(E) \cap \mathrm{Var}_s = \emptyset$ and $\mathrm{ResVars}(R_j) \subseteq \mathrm{Var}_s$, or $E \subseteq R_j$).

Assume $G \uplus E$ is non-tautological. Then there is an $\mathrm{mRes}$ derivation of $G \uplus E$ in at most $q+1$ steps satisfying (P2) and (P3), with
$$\mathrm{ResVars}(G \uplus E) \subseteq \bigl(\mathrm{ResVars}(L_0) \cup \bigcup_{j=1}^q \mathrm{ResVars}(R_j) \cup \{\mathrm{var}(\ell_1),\dots,\mathrm{var}(\ell_q)\}\bigr) \setminus \mathrm{var}(G \uplus E),$$
where $\mathrm{ResVars}(L_0)$ is omitted whenever $J := \{j \in \{1,\dots,q\} : \neg \ell_j \in G\}$ is nonempty.

*Proof.* Let $J := \{j \in \{1,\dots,q\} : \neg \ell_j \in G\}$.
- **Case 1 ($J \neq \emptyset$):** Let $j_0 := \max J$. Weakening $R_{j_0}$ by $E$ gives $S_{j_0} := G \uplus E \uplus \ell_{j_0+1} \uplus \dots \uplus \ell_q$ (contracting the duplicate $\neg \ell_{j_0} \in G$). Notice $S_{j_0}$ is non-tautological: $G \uplus E$ is non-tautological, and for every $j > j_0$, $\neg \ell_j \notin G$ and $\mathrm{var}(\ell_j) \notin \mathrm{var}(E)$ (since $\mathrm{var}(E) \cap \mathrm{var}(H) = \emptyset$). Moreover, $\mathrm{ResVars}(S_{j_0}) = \mathrm{ResVars}(R_{j_0})$ is disjoint from $\mathrm{var}(G \uplus E)$ (since $\mathrm{var}(E) \cap \mathrm{ResVars}(R_{j_0}) = \emptyset$) and from $\{\mathrm{var}(\ell_{j_0+1}), \dots, \mathrm{var}(\ell_q)\}$ (since $\ell_j \in R_{j_0}$ for $j > j_0$ and $R_{j_0}$ satisfies (P3)).
- **Case 2 ($J = \emptyset$):** Set $j_0 := 0$ and $S_0 := L_0 = G \uplus E \uplus \ell_1 \uplus \dots \uplus \ell_q$, which is non-tautological (as $\neg\ell_j \notin G$ and $\mathrm{var}(\ell_j) \notin \mathrm{var}(E)$ for all $j$) and satisfies (P3) (so $\mathrm{ResVars}(S_0)$ is disjoint from $\mathrm{var}(G \uplus E) \cup \{\mathrm{var}(\ell_1),\dots,\mathrm{var}(\ell_q)\}$).

For $j = j_0 + 1, \dots, q$, resolve $S_{j-1} = G \uplus E \uplus \ell_j \uplus \dots \uplus \ell_q$ with $R_j$ on $x_j := \mathrm{var}(\ell_j)$ and contract duplicates to obtain $S_j = G \uplus E \uplus \ell_{j+1} \uplus \dots \uplus \ell_q$:
- Since $j > j_0$, $\neg\ell_j \notin G$ and $\mathrm{var}(\ell_j) \notin \mathrm{var}(E)$, so $S_{j-1}$ and $S_j$ are non-tautological.
- If $\ell_j \in G$, then $R_j$ contains both $\ell_j$ and $\neg\ell_j$ (so $R_j$ is tautological, derived in $1$ step from the axiom $x_j \uplus \neg x_j$ with $\mathrm{ResVars}(R_j)=\emptyset$), and $x_j \in \mathrm{var}(\operatorname{red}(S_j))$ (specifically $\ell_j \in \operatorname{red}(S_j)$), so in $\pi'$ the step $S_{j-1}, R_j \to S_j$ deletes $R_j$ and becomes repetition/weakening from $S_{j-1}$ alone (preserving (P2)–(P3) with $\mathrm{ResVars}(S_j) = \mathrm{ResVars}(S_{j-1})$).
- If $\ell_j \notin G$, then $R_j$ is non-tautological (as $G$ is non-tautological, $\neg\ell_j \notin G$, and $\neg\ell_u \notin G$ for all $u > j > j_0$) and $x_j \notin \mathrm{var}(G \uplus E)$, so $S_{j-1}, R_j \to S_j$ is variable-eliminating on $x_j$. By (P3) on $S_{j-1}$ (which contains $\ell_j$) and $R_j$ (which contains $\neg\ell_j$), $x_j \notin \mathrm{ResVars}(S_{j-1}) \cup \mathrm{ResVars}(R_j)$, so (P2) holds at $S_j$. Furthermore, every variable $z \in \mathrm{var}(S_j)$ belongs to $\mathrm{var}(S_{j-1})$ (and if $z \in \mathrm{var}(G)\cup\{\mathrm{var}(\ell_{j+1}),\dots,\mathrm{var}(\ell_q)\}$, also to $\mathrm{var}(R_j)$, while if $z \in \mathrm{var}(E)$, $z \notin \mathrm{ResVars}(R_j)$ by hypothesis), so $z \notin \mathrm{ResVars}(S_j)$, preserving (P3).

At $j = q$, $S_q = G \uplus E$ satisfies (P2) and (P3), using at most $q+1$ steps. $\square$

*(Remark: In Case 5 below, when $J = \emptyset$, $L_0 = G \uplus c$ is obtained by weakening $c$ to $G \uplus c$ so $\mathrm{ResVars}(L_0) = \emptyset$; if $\ell_1 \in G$, then $x_1 \in \operatorname{red}(S_1)$ so $S_0, R_1 \to S_1$ reduces in $\pi'$ to weakening from $S_0 = L_0$ alone, keeping $\mathrm{ResVars}(S_1) = \emptyset$.)*

## 4. Bottom-Up Construction and Verification of (P0)–(P3) at Every Node Type

We construct $\pi$ bottom-up in post-order on $T$. Let $(t,\alpha,A)$ be an inconsistent state such that $c_t^{\mathrm{int}} \neq \bot$ for all $c \in A \setminus P_t$, fix $\vec C \in \prod_{c\in A\setminus P_t}\mathcal C_1(c_t^{\mathrm{int}})$, and let $M := M(t,\alpha,A,\vec C)$. If $M$ is tautological, derive it in $1$ step from an axiom $v \uplus \neg v$ as noted in Section 3. Assume henceforth that $M$ is non-tautological.

### Case 1: Leaf node $t$
$\chi(t) = \emptyset$; $(t,\emptyset,\emptyset)$ is consistent, so there are no inconsistent states.

### Case 2: Introduce-clause node $t$ introducing $c$ with child $t'$
Note $\mathrm{Var}_t = \mathrm{Var}_{t'}$, $\chi_v(t) = \chi_v(t')$, and by Lemma `lem:introduce-clause-local`, $\mathrm{var}(c) \cap \mathrm{Var}_t = \mathrm{var}(c) \cap \chi_v(t)$.
- **(a) $c \notin A$:** $(t',\alpha,A)$ is inconsistent. Take $M' = M(t',\alpha,A,\vec C)$ from $t'$. If $c \in P_t$ or $\alpha$ satisfies $c$, then $M = M'$ (0 steps). Otherwise $c \notin P_t$ and $\alpha$ does not satisfy $c$, so $M = M' \uplus c_t^{\mathrm{int}}$. Since $\mathrm{var}(c_t^{\mathrm{int}}) \subseteq \chi_v(t)$, weakening $M'$ by $c_t^{\mathrm{int}}$ introduces only variables in $\chi_v(t)$, which are disjoint from $\mathrm{ResVars}(M') \subseteq \mathrm{Var}_{t'}\setminus\chi_v(t')$ by (P1). Hence (P0)–(P3) hold ($1$ step).
- **(b) $c \in A$ and $\alpha$ satisfies $c$:** By Lemma `lem:introduce-clause-zero`(b), $(t',\alpha,A\setminus\{c\})$ is inconsistent. Take $M'$ from $(t',\alpha,A\setminus\{c\},\vec C|_{A\setminus\{c\}})$. Since $\alpha$ satisfies $c$, $M = M'$ if $c \in P_t$, and $M = M' \uplus C_c$ if $c \notin P_t$. By Lemma `lem:introduce-clause-local`, $\mathrm{var}(c) \cap \mathrm{Var}_t = \mathrm{var}(c) \cap \chi_v(t)$, so $\mathrm{var}(C_c) \subseteq \chi_v(t)$. Weakening $M'$ by $C_c$ adds only variables in $\chi_v(t)$, preserving (P0)–(P3) ($1$ step).
- **(c) $c \in A$ and $\alpha$ does not satisfy $c$:**
  - If $c \in P_t$: since $\mathrm{var}(c_t^{\mathrm{int}}) \subseteq \chi_v(t)$ and $\alpha$ does not satisfy $c$, every literal of $c_t^{\mathrm{int}}$ is falsified by $\alpha$, hence belongs to $C_\alpha$. Thus weakening the initial clause $c = c_t^{\mathrm{int}} \uplus c_t^{\mathrm{ext}}$ to $M$ gives $\mathrm{ResVars}(M) = \emptyset$, satisfying (P0)–(P3) ($1$ step).
  - If $c \notin P_t$: since $c_t^{\mathrm{int}} \neq \bot$, $C_c \in \mathcal C_1(c_t^{\mathrm{int}})$ starts with $\neg \ell_i$ for some $\ell_i \in c_t^{\mathrm{int}}$. Because $\mathrm{var}(\ell_i) \in \chi_v(t)$ and $\alpha$ does not satisfy $c$, $\ell_i \in C_\alpha$. Weakening the axiom $\mathrm{var}(\ell_i) \uplus \neg\mathrm{var}(\ell_i)$ to $M$ has $\mathrm{ResVars}(M) = \emptyset$, satisfying (P0)–(P3) ($1$ step).

### Case 3: Introduce-variable node $t$ introducing $x$ with child $t'$
Let $\alpha' := \alpha|_{\chi_v(t')}$, let $\ell = x$ if $\alpha(x)=1$ else $\neg x$ (so $C_\alpha = C_{\alpha'} \uplus \neg \ell$), and let $A_x := \{c \in A : \ell \notin c\}$. By Lemma `lem:introduce-var-zero`, $(t',\alpha',A_x)$ is inconsistent. Note $P_t = P_{t'}$.

Every $c \in (A \setminus A_x) \setminus P_t$ contains $\ell$ (and not $\neg\ell$, since $F$ has no tautological clauses). Write $c_t^{\mathrm{int}} = \ell_1 \lor \dots \lor \ell_{p-1} \lor \ell \lor \ell_{p+1} \lor \dots \lor \ell_r$ in post-order of $\mathrm{forg}$, where $\ell = \ell_p$.
- If $C_c \in \mathcal C_1(c_t^{\mathrm{int}})$ starts at index $i < p$, then $\ell \in C_c$ while $\neg \ell \in C_\alpha$, making $M$ tautological (already handled). Thus for every $c \in (A \setminus A_x) \setminus P_t$, $C_c$ starts at index $i \ge p$.
- Likewise, for any $c \in A_x \setminus P_t$: since $\ell \notin c$, either $\neg\ell \notin c$ (so $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}} = c_t^{\mathrm{int}} \neq \bot$ and $C_c \in \mathcal C_1(c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}})$ directly) or $\neg\ell \in c_t^{\mathrm{int}}$ at some position $q$. In the latter case, if $C_c$ starts at index $i = q$ (including the case $c_t^{\mathrm{int}} = \neg\ell$, where $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}} = \bot$), then $\ell \in C_c$ while $\neg\ell \in C_\alpha$, making $M$ tautological (already handled); if $i > q$, then by Observation 1(1) every literal of $C_c$ lies in $\chi_v(t')$, so $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}} \neq \bot$ and $C_c \in \mathcal C_1(c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}})$; and if $i < q$, then $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}} \neq \bot$ and $C_c^{(t')} := C_c \setminus \{\neg\ell\} \in \mathcal C_1(c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}})$ (and $\neg\ell$ is restored into $M$ when weakening by $\neg\ell \in C_\alpha$ below). Let $C_c^{(t')} := C_c \setminus \{\neg\ell\} \in \mathcal C_1(c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}})$ in all cases for $c \in A_x \setminus P_t$.
- Let $A_{\mathrm{base}} := A_x \cup \{c \in (A \setminus A_x) \setminus P_t : \text{choice for } c \text{ has } i > p\}$. By upward monotonicity, $(t',\alpha',A_{\mathrm{base}})$ is inconsistent. Notice that for $c \in A_{\mathrm{base}} \setminus A_x$, all literals in $C_c$ ($i > p$) have variables with $\mathrm{forg} > \mathrm{forg}(x)$, hence lie in $\chi_v(t')$ by Observation 1(1), so $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}} \neq \bot$ and $C_c$ is already an element of $\mathcal C_1(c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}})$ (namely at index $i-1$).
- Let $W_p := \{c \in (A \setminus A_x) \setminus P_t : \text{choice for } c \text{ has } i = p\}$ and let $S_{\mathrm{sat}} := \{c \in (\chi_c(t) \setminus P_t \setminus A) : \ell \in c,\ c \in U_{\alpha'}(\chi_c(t'))\}$. Define the unified elimination list
  $$W := \{c \in (W_p \cap U_{\alpha'}(\chi_c(t'))) \cup S_{\mathrm{sat}} : c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t') \neq \bot\},$$
  ordered as $c_1, \dots, c_s$. For each $c \in W$, $c \in U_{\alpha'}(\chi_c(t'))$ so every literal of $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}} \cap \mathrm{lits}(\chi_v(t'))$ already lies in $C_{\alpha'}$; moreover, by Observation 1(1) ($\mathrm{Var}_{t'}\setminus\chi_v(t') = \mathrm{Var}_t\setminus\chi_v(t)$ precedes $\chi_v(t) = \chi_v(t') \cup \{x\}$), $H_c := c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t') = \ell_1 \lor \dots \lor \ell_{p_c}$ ($p_c \ge 1$) is a nonempty prefix of $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}}$ and every remaining literal of $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}}$ lies in $\mathrm{lits}(\chi_v(t')) \cap C_{\alpha'}$. (In particular, for any $c \in W_p \setminus W$, either $c \notin U_{\alpha'}$ — contributing nothing to $\mathcal C_0$ at $t'$ — or $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t') = \bot$ so $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}} \subseteq C_{\alpha'}$; and for any $c \in S_{\mathrm{sat}}$ with $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t') = \bot$, $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}} \subseteq C_{\alpha'}$ already.)

By reverse induction on $d = s, s-1, \dots, 0$, for every assignment of choices $k_j \in \{1,\dots,p_{c_j}\}$ to $c_j$ for $1 \le j \le d$ and every subset $U \subseteq \{c_1,\dots,c_d\}$, we derive the multiclause at $t'$ in which clauses in $A_{\mathrm{base}} \cup U$ use $\mathcal C_1$ (specifically $C_c^{(t')}$ for $c \in A_x \setminus P_t$, $C_c$ for $c \in A_{\mathrm{base}}\setminus A_x$, and the chosen $k_j$-th clause of $\mathcal C_1(c_{j,t'}^{\mathrm{int}})$ for $c_j \in U$), clauses in $\{c_1,\dots,c_d\} \setminus U$ use $\mathcal C_0$ (yielding $c_{j,t'}^{\mathrm{int}}$, since every $c_j \in W \subseteq U_{\alpha'}$), and each already-eliminated clause $c_{j}$ ($j > d$) contributes nothing beyond $C_{\alpha'}$ (its $\chi_v(t')$-tail already lies in $C_{\alpha'}$):
- For $d = s$: this is a directly available multiclause at $t'$ for $(t',\alpha',A_{\mathrm{base}} \cup U)$, satisfying (P1)–(P3).
- For $d \to d-1$: to eliminate $H_{c_d} = \ell_1 \lor \dots \lor \ell_{p_{c_d}}$ of $c_d$, the left premise $L_0$ is the $d$-level multiclause with $c_d \notin U$ (contributing $c_{d,t'}^{\mathrm{int}} = H_{c_d} \uplus \mathrm{tail}_{c_d}$ with $\mathrm{tail}_{c_d} \subseteq C_{\alpha'} \subseteq G$), and each right premise $R_k$ ($1 \le k \le p_{c_d}$) is the $d$-level multiclause with $c_d \in U$ and $k_d = k$ (contributing $\neg \ell_k \uplus \ell_{k+1} \uplus \dots \uplus \ell_{p_{c_d}} \uplus \mathrm{tail}_{c_d}$). By Lemma 2 (with $E = \bot$, $H = H_{c_d}$), we derive the $(d-1)$-level multiclause, preserving (P1)–(P3) at $t'$ (if the target at level $d-1$ is tautological, derive it in $1$ step from an axiom instead).

At $d = 0$, we obtain the multiclause $M_{t'}$ over $t'$ with (P1)–(P3) and $\mathrm{ResVars}(M_{t'}) \subseteq \mathrm{Var}_{t'} \setminus \chi_v(t')$. Every literal of $M_{t'}$ lies in $M$ (note that $(A \setminus A_x) \cap P_t \cap U_\alpha = \emptyset$ since $\ell \in c$ for all $c \in A \setminus A_x$), and the only literals of $M$ possibly missing from $M_{t'}$ are:
- $\neg \ell \in C_\alpha$ (variable $x$),
- $\mathrm{tail}_c := C_c \setminus \{\neg\ell\} \subseteq \mathrm{lits}(\chi_v(t'))$ for $c \in W_p \setminus U_{\alpha'}$, and
- for $c \in (\chi_c(t) \setminus P_t \setminus A) \cap U_\alpha$, the literal on $x$ in $c_t^{\mathrm{int}}$ if any (variable $x$).
All other literals of $M_{t'}$ already match $M$: for $c \in A_x \cap P_t \cap U_{\alpha'}$, $c \in U_\alpha$ too, and either $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{ext}} = c_t^{\mathrm{ext}}$ or $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{ext}} = c_t^{\mathrm{ext}} \uplus \neg \ell$ where $\neg\ell \in C_\alpha$. Thus $M_{t'} \subseteq M$, and weakening $M_{t'}$ to $M$ adds only variables in $\chi_v(t') \cup \{x\} = \chi_v(t)$, completely disjoint from $\mathrm{ResVars}(M_{t'}) \subseteq \mathrm{Var}_{t'} \setminus \chi_v(t')$, preserving (P0)–(P3).

Since $1 + |H_c| \ge 2$ for every $c \in W$, summing the $\le |H_{c_d}| + 1$ Lemma 2 steps over all levels $d = 1, \dots, s$ and all subsets/choices plus the final weakening step gives a total step count per choice tuple at $t$ of at most
$$\sum_{d=1}^{s} \prod_{j=1}^{d} (1 + |H_{c_j}|) + 1 \le 2 \prod_{c \in W} (1 + |H_c|) + 1 \le 2 \prod_{c \in \chi_c(t')\setminus P_{t'}} (1 + \rho_{\mathcal P}(c)) + 1 \le 2^{w+2} + 1$$
(by Lemma 1 at $t'$).

### Case 4: Forget-variable node $t$ forgetting $x$ with child $t'$
Note $\mathrm{Var}_t = \mathrm{Var}_{t'}$, $\chi_c(t) = \chi_c(t')$, $P_t = P_{t'}$, and $c_t^{\mathrm{int}} = c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}}$, $c_t^{\mathrm{ext}} = c_{t'}^{\mathrm{ext}}$ for all $c \in \chi_c(t)$. Let $\alpha_b := \alpha \cup \{x \mapsto b\}$ ($b \in \{0,1\}$); by Lemma `lem:forget-var-zero`, both $(t',\alpha_0,A)$ and $(t',\alpha_1,A)$ are inconsistent. Let $M_b := M(t',\alpha_b,A,\vec C)$, already derived with (P0)–(P3) at $t'$.
- Notice that $c \in B_t(A) \cap U_\alpha(A)$ is in $U_{\alpha_0}(A) \cup U_{\alpha_1}(A)$ (if $x \in c$, $c \in U_{\alpha_0}$; if $\neg x \in c$, $c \in U_{\alpha_1}$; if $x\notin\mathrm{var}(c)$, $c$ is in both), and similarly for $(\chi_c(t)\setminus P_t\setminus A)\cap U_\alpha$.
- **Subcase 4a ($x \in M$):** Since $M$ is non-tautological, $\neg x \notin M$. Because $\alpha_0 = \alpha \cup \{x \mapsto 0\}$, we have $C_{\alpha_0} = C_\alpha \uplus x \subseteq M$ (as $x \in M$) and $U_{\alpha_0}(S) = \{d \in U_\alpha(S) : \neg x \notin d\} \subseteq U_\alpha(S)$ for any $S \subseteq \chi_c(t)$. Comparing $M_0$ and $M$ term by term: every $c \in A \setminus P_t$ contributes the same $C_c$ to both $M_0$ and $M$; every $d \in (\chi_c(t)\setminus P_t \setminus A)\cap U_{\alpha_0}$ also lies in $U_\alpha$ and contributes $d_t^{\mathrm{int}} \subseteq M$; and every $d \in B_t(A)\cap U_{\alpha_0}$ also lies in $U_\alpha$ and contributes $d_t^{\mathrm{ext}} \subseteq M$. Thus $M_0 \subseteq M$ directly. Conversely, no $d \in (\chi_c(t)\setminus P_t\setminus A)\cap U_\alpha$ can contain $\neg x$ (otherwise $\neg x \in d_t^{\mathrm{int}} \subseteq M$), so $U_{\alpha_0}$ agrees with $U_\alpha$ on $\chi_c(t)\setminus P_t\setminus A$, and the only literals of $M$ possibly missing from $M_0$ are $d_t^{\mathrm{ext}}$ for $d \in B_t(A)\cap U_\alpha$ with $\neg x \in d$. Weakening $M_0$ to $M$ introduces only variables outside $\mathrm{Var}_t$, yielding $M$ with zero resolutions ($1$ step) and preserving (P0)–(P3).
- **Subcase 4b ($\neg x \in M$):** Symmetric to Subcase 4a using $M_1$.
- **Subcase 4c ($x \notin \mathrm{var}(M)$):** Then $M_0 = G_0 \uplus x$ (with $\neg x \notin G_0$) and $M_1 = G_1 \uplus \neg x$ (with $x \notin G_1$), and $G_0 \cup G_1 = M$. Resolving $M_0$ and $M_1$ on $x$ produces $M$ in $1$ step:
  - Because $x \notin \mathrm{var}(M)$, this step is variable-eliminating on $x$.
  - By (P3) on $M_0$ ($x \in M_0$) and $M_1$ ($\neg x \in M_1$), $x \notin \mathrm{ResVars}(M_0) \cup \mathrm{ResVars}(M_1)$, so (P2) holds.
  - Every variable $y \in \mathrm{var}(M) \cap \mathrm{Var}_t$ originates from $C_\alpha$, $C_c$ ($c \in A \setminus P_t$), or $d_t^{\mathrm{int}}$ ($d \in (\chi_c(t)\setminus P_t \setminus A)\cap U_\alpha$, where $x \notin \mathrm{var}(d)$ since $x \notin \mathrm{var}(M)$) and differs from $x$, hence lies in $\mathrm{var}(M_0) \cap \mathrm{var}(M_1)$ and thus outside $\mathrm{ResVars}(M_0) \cup \mathrm{ResVars}(M_1)$ by (P3). Every variable $y \in \mathrm{var}(M) \setminus \mathrm{Var}_t$ lies outside $\mathrm{ResVars}(M) \subseteq \mathrm{ResVars}(M_0) \cup \mathrm{ResVars}(M_1) \cup \{x\} \subseteq \mathrm{Var}_t \setminus \chi_v(t)$ by (P1). Hence (P1) and (P3) hold.

### Case 5: Forget-clause node $t$ forgetting $c$ with child $t'$
Note $\mathrm{Var}_t = \mathrm{Var}_{t'}$, $\chi_v(t) = \chi_v(t')$, and by Lemma `lem:forget-clause-zero`, $(t',\alpha,A\cup\{c\})$ is inconsistent. By Lemma `lem:forget-clause-internal`, $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{int}} = c$ and $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{ext}} = \bot$.
- If $c \in P_{t'}$: then $c_{t'}^{\mathrm{ext}} = \bot$, so $M(t',\alpha,A\cup\{c\},\vec C) = M$ directly ($0$ steps), preserving (P0)–(P3).
- If $c \notin P_{t'}$: write $c = \ell_1 \lor \dots \lor \ell_r$ in post-order of $\mathrm{forg}$. Let $G := M$ (non-tautological). For each $j = 1,\dots,r$, let $R_j := M(t',\alpha,A\cup\{c\},(\vec C, C_j))$ where $C_j = \neg\ell_j \uplus \ell_{j+1} \uplus \dots \uplus \ell_r \in \mathcal C_1(c)$. Notice $R_j = G \uplus C_j$ has already been derived at $t'$ (satisfying (P1)–(P3) whenever non-tautological, and obtained in $1$ step from an axiom with $\mathrm{ResVars}=\emptyset$ when tautological, which occurs iff $\ell_j \in G$ or $\neg\ell_u \in G$ for some $u > j$). Let $J := \{i \in \{1,\dots,r\} : \neg\ell_i \in G\}$.
  - If $J \neq \emptyset$ and $j_0 := \max J$, then $\neg\ell_u \notin G$ for all $u > j_0$ and $\ell_{j_0} \notin G$ (as $G$ is non-tautological), so $R_{j_0}$ is non-tautological; for each $j > j_0$, $\neg\ell_j \notin G$ (and $R_j$ is either non-tautological if $\ell_j \notin G$ or tautological if $\ell_j \in G$, both handled by Lemma 2).
  - If $J = \emptyset$, obtain $L_0 := G \uplus c$ by weakening the initial clause $c$ by $G$ ($1$ step, $\mathrm{ResVars}(L_0) = \emptyset$), and for each $1 \le j \le r$, $R_j$ is either non-tautological (if $\ell_j \notin G$) or tautological (if $\ell_j \in G$).
  Thus **Lemma 2** applies directly (with $E = \bot$, $H = c$) to derive $G = M$ in at most $r+1 \le k+1$ steps, preserving (P0)–(P3).

### Case 6: Join node $t_0$ with ordered children $t_1, t_2$
Recall $\chi(t_0) = \chi(t_1) = \chi(t_2)$ and $\mathrm{Var}_{t_0} = \mathrm{Var}_{t_1} \cup \mathrm{Var}_{t_2}$ with $(\mathrm{Var}_{t_1}\setminus\chi_v(t_0)) \cap (\mathrm{Var}_{t_2}\setminus\chi_v(t_0)) = \emptyset$. Also $B_0 := P_{t_0} \cap A = B_1 \sqcup B_2$ where $B_i := P_{t_i} \cap A$ ($i=1,2$). Let $B_0^{\mathrm{unsat}} := B_0 \cap U_\alpha(A)$ and $B_0^{\mathrm{sat}} := B_0 \setminus B_0^{\mathrm{unsat}}$. Partition $B_0^{\mathrm{unsat}}$ into:
- $B_{1,\mathrm{pure}} := \{d \in B_1 \cap U_\alpha : d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t_0) = \bot\}$ and $B_{2,\mathrm{pure}} := \{d \in B_2 \cap U_\alpha : d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t_0) = \bot\}$, and
- $B_0^{\mathrm{split}} := B_0^{\mathrm{unsat}} \setminus (B_{1,\mathrm{pure}} \cup B_{2,\mathrm{pure}}) = \{d_1,\dots,d_p\}$.

Note that for every $d \in B_{i,\mathrm{pure}}$ ($i \in \{1,2\}$), $d_{t_i}^{\mathrm{ext}} = d_{t_0}^{\mathrm{ext}}$ and $d_{t_{3-i}}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t_0) = \bot$, while for every $d \in B_0^{\mathrm{split}}$, if $d \in B_2$ then $d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t_0) \neq \bot$ (so $d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}} \neq \bot$ and $\mathcal C_1(d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}}) \neq \emptyset$), and if $d \in B_1$ then $d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t_0) \neq \bot$ (so $d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}} \neq \bot$ and $\mathcal C_1(d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}}) \neq \emptyset$).

Route every off-path clause $c \in A \setminus P_{t_0}$ deterministically according to the leading literal $\neg \ell_i$ of $C_c \in \mathcal C_1(c_{t_0}^{\mathrm{int}})$:
$$A_1^{\mathrm{off}} := \{c \in A \setminus P_{t_0} : \mathrm{var}(\ell_i) \in \mathrm{Var}_{t_1} \setminus \chi_v(t_0)\}, \qquad A_2^{\mathrm{off}} := (A \setminus P_{t_0}) \setminus A_1^{\mathrm{off}} = \{c \in A \setminus P_{t_0} : \mathrm{var}(\ell_i) \in \mathrm{Var}_{t_2}\}.$$
By Observation 1(2) ($y_1 < y_2 < x$), if $c \in A_2^{\mathrm{off}}$, then $\mathrm{var}(\ell_i) \in \mathrm{Var}_{t_2}$, so **every** variable of $C_c$ lies in $\mathrm{Var}_{t_2}$, and hence $c_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}} \neq \bot$, $C_c \in \mathcal C_1(c_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}})$, and $\mathrm{var}(C_c) \cap (\mathrm{Var}_{t_1} \setminus \chi_v(t_0)) = \emptyset$.
Conversely, if $c \in A_1^{\mathrm{off}}$, then $\mathrm{var}(\ell_i) \in \mathrm{Var}_{t_1}\setminus \chi_v(t_0)$, so $c_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}} \neq \bot$, restricting $C_c$ to $\mathrm{Var}_{t_1}$ yields $C_c^{(1)} := C_c \cap \mathrm{lits}(\mathrm{Var}_{t_1}) \in \mathcal C_1(c_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}})$ with remaining tail literals in $c_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t_0)$, and moreover $c_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}} \subseteq C_c$ (because every literal of $c_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}}$ has variable in $\mathrm{Var}_{t_2} \setminus \chi_v(t_0)$ or $\chi_v(t_0)$, hence follows $\ell_i$ in post-order by Observation 1(2)).

For each $h \in \{0,1,\dots,p\}$, every partition $A_1^b \sqcup A_2^b = \{d_1,\dots,d_h\}$, and every choice tuple $(\vec C_{b2}, \vec C_{b1})$ where $\vec C_{b2} = (C_d)_{d \in A_1^b \cap B_2} \in \prod \mathcal C_1(d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}})$ and $\vec C_{b1} = (C_d)_{d \in A_2^b \cap B_1} \in \prod \mathcal C_1(d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}})$, define the intermediate target multiclause $T(h, A_1^b, A_2^b, \vec C_{b2}, \vec C_{b1})$ as:
$$C_\alpha \uplus \biguplus_{d \in (A_1^b \cap B_1) \cup B_{1,\mathrm{pure}}} d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{ext}} \uplus \biguplus_{d \in A_1^b \cap B_2} C_d \uplus \biguplus_{d \in (A_2^b \cap B_2) \cup B_{2,\mathrm{pure}}} d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{ext}} \uplus \biguplus_{d \in A_2^b \cap B_1} C_d \uplus \biguplus_{d \in \{d_{h+1},\dots,d_p\}} d_{t_0}^{\mathrm{ext}} \uplus \biguplus_{c \in A\setminus P_{t_0}} C_c \uplus \biguplus_{c \in (\chi_c(t_0)\setminus P_{t_0}\setminus A)\cap U_\alpha} c_{t_0}^{\mathrm{int}}.$$
We prove by reverse induction on $h = p, p-1, \dots, 0$ that every such $T$ has an $\mathrm{mRes}$ derivation satisfying (P1)–(P3) (with $\mathrm{ResVars}(T) \subseteq \mathrm{Var}_{t_0} \setminus \chi_v(t_0)$):

- **Base ($h = p$):** Let $A_1 := A_1^{\mathrm{off}} \cup A_1^b \cup B_{1,\mathrm{pure}}$ and $A_2 := A_2^{\mathrm{off}} \cup A_2^b \cup B_{2,\mathrm{pure}}$. Note $A_1 \cup A_2 = A \setminus B_0^{\mathrm{sat}}$, and since $\alpha$ satisfies $B_0^{\mathrm{sat}}$, $(t_0, \alpha, A \setminus B_0^{\mathrm{sat}})$ is inconsistent. By Lemma `lem:join-zero-recursion`, either $(t_1, \alpha, A_1)$ or $(t_2, \alpha, A_2)$ is inconsistent. If $T$ is tautological, derive it from an axiom (`ResVars = \emptyset`). Otherwise:
  - If $(t_1,\alpha,A_1)$ is inconsistent: let $D_1 := ((P_{t_2} \cap \chi_c(t_0)) \setminus A) \cap U_\alpha(\chi_c(t_0))$ and $E_1 := D_1 \cup (A_2^{\mathrm{off}} \cap U_\alpha(\chi_c(t_0)))$. Observe that $E_1 \subseteq (\chi_c(t_1) \setminus P_{t_1}) \cap U_\alpha$ (since $P_{t_0}\setminus P_{t_1} = P_{t_2}\cap\chi_c(t_0)$ and $A_2^{\mathrm{off}} \subseteq A \setminus P_{t_0}$), and the remaining clauses in $(\chi_c(t_1) \setminus P_{t_1} \setminus A_1) \cap U_\alpha$ are precisely $(A_2^b \cap B_2) \cup B_{2,\mathrm{pure}} \cup ((\chi_c(t_0)\setminus P_{t_0}\setminus A)\cap U_\alpha)$. For each $c \in E_1$ with $c_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}} \neq \bot$, $M_1(A_1)$ contains $c_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}}$ via $\mathcal C_0$, whereas $c_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t_0)$ is absent from $T$ (for $c \in D_1$, $c \in P_{t_0}\setminus A$; for $c \in A_2^{\mathrm{off}}$, $T$ has $C_c \in \mathcal C_1(c_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}})$ whose variables lie entirely in $\mathrm{Var}_{t_2}$). Eliminating $c_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t_0)$ for each $c \in E_1$ (with $c_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t_0) \neq \bot$) one by one via Lemma 2 (resolving $A_1$-side $\mathcal C_0$ against $(A_1\cup\{c\})$-side $\mathcal C_1(c_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}})$, noting that any $\chi_v(t_0)$-literals of $c$ already lie in $C_\alpha$ since $c \in U_\alpha$, and that $\prod_{c\in E_1}(1+|c_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}}\setminus\chi_v(t_0)|) \le 2^{w+1}$ by Lemma 1 at $t_1$) produces a multiclause $M_1'$ over $t_1$ with $\mathrm{ResVars}(M_1') \subseteq \mathrm{Var}_{t_1}\setminus\chi_v(t_0)$ satisfying (P1)–(P3) and containing no literals of $c_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t_0)$ for any $c \in E_1$. Check every literal in $T \setminus M_1'$ (and verify $M_1' \subseteq T$):
    - for $d \in (A_1^b \cap B_1) \cup B_{1,\mathrm{pure}}$: $M_1'$ has $d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{ext}} \subseteq T$ ($d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{ext}} = d_{t_0}^{\mathrm{ext}}$ when $d \in B_{1,\mathrm{pure}}$);
    - for $d \in A_1^b \cap B_2$: $d \in A_1 \setminus P_{t_1}$ with $d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}} \neq \bot$, so $M_1'$ already has $C_d \subseteq T$;
    - for $d \in (A_2^b \cap B_2) \cup B_{2,\mathrm{pure}}$: $d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{ext}} = d_{t_0}^{\mathrm{ext}} \uplus (d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t_0))$; since $d \notin A_1$ and $t_1 \notin P_d$, $M_1'$ already contains $d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}}$ via $\mathcal C_0(d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}})$ (note $d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}} \cap \chi_v(t_0) \subseteq C_\alpha$, and for $d \in B_{2,\mathrm{pure}}$ we have $d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{ext}} = d_{t_0}^{\mathrm{ext}}$), so only $d_{t_0}^{\mathrm{ext}}$ (variables outside $\mathrm{Var}_{t_0}$) is missing;
    - for $c \in A_1^{\mathrm{off}}$: $M_1'$ has $C_c^{(1)} = C_c \cap \mathrm{lits}(\mathrm{Var}_{t_1})$, so missing literals are in $c_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t_0) \subseteq \mathrm{Var}_{t_2}\setminus\chi_v(t_0)$;
    - for $c \in A_2^{\mathrm{off}}$: $C_c$ has variables only in $\mathrm{Var}_{t_2}$;
    - for $d \in A_2^b \cap B_1$: $C_d \in \mathcal C_1(d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}})$ has variables only in $\mathrm{Var}_{t_2}$;
    - for $c \in (\chi_c(t_0)\setminus P_{t_0}\setminus A)\cap U_\alpha$: $M_1'$ already has $c_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}}$ via $\mathcal C_0$, so only $c_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}}\setminus \chi_v(t_0)$ is missing.
    Thus $M_1' \subseteq T$ and **every** variable added when weakening $M_1'$ to $T$ lies in $(\mathrm{var}(F)\setminus \mathrm{Var}_{t_0}) \cup (\mathrm{Var}_{t_2}\setminus\chi_v(t_0))$, which is completely disjoint from $\mathrm{ResVars}(M_1') \subseteq \mathrm{Var}_{t_1}\setminus\chi_v(t_0)$, preserving (P1)–(P3).
  - Symmetrically, if $(t_2,\alpha,A_2)$ is inconsistent: eliminate $d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t_0)$ for each $d \in D_2 := ((P_{t_1}\cap\chi_c(t_0))\setminus A)\cap U_\alpha(\chi_c(t_0))$ (with $d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}}\setminus \chi_v(t_0) \neq \bot$) via Lemma 2 at $t_2$ (at most $\prod_{d\in D_2}(1+|d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}}\setminus\chi_v(t_0)|) \le 2^{w+1}$ steps) to obtain $M_2'$ with $\mathrm{ResVars}(M_2') \subseteq \mathrm{Var}_{t_2}\setminus\chi_v(t_0)$. For $c \in A_1^{\mathrm{off}} \cap U_\alpha$, since $c \notin A_2$ and $t_2 \notin P_c$, $M_2'$ already contains $c_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}}$ via $\mathcal C_0$, and we checked above that $c_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}} \subseteq C_c \subseteq T$ (and for $c \in A_1^{\mathrm{off}} \setminus U_\alpha$, $c_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}}$ is not added to $M_2'$). Likewise, for $c \in (\chi_c(t_0)\setminus P_{t_0}\setminus A)\cap U_\alpha$, $M_2'$ contains $c_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}} \subseteq c_{t_0}^{\mathrm{int}}$, and for $d \in (A_1^b \cap B_1) \cup B_{1,\mathrm{pure}}$, $M_2'$ contains $d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}}$ via $\mathcal C_0$ (with $d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}}\cap\chi_v(t_0)\subseteq C_\alpha$, and $d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}}\setminus\chi_v(t_0)\subseteq d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{ext}}$ for $d\in A_1^b\cap B_1$, or $=\bot$ for $d\in B_{1,\mathrm{pure}}$), so only $d_{t_0}^{\mathrm{ext}}$ is missing from $d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{ext}}$. Every remaining missing literal ($C_c \cap \mathrm{lits}(\mathrm{Var}_{t_1})$ for $c\in A_1^{\mathrm{off}}$, $C_d$ for $d \in A_1^b \cap B_2$, and $c_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}}\setminus\chi_v(t_0)$ for $c\in (\chi_c(t_0)\setminus P_{t_0}\setminus A)\cap U_\alpha$) has variables only in $\mathrm{Var}_{t_1}$ or outside $\mathrm{Var}_{t_0}$, completely disjoint from $\mathrm{ResVars}(M_2') \subseteq \mathrm{Var}_{t_2}\setminus\chi_v(t_0)$. Thus $M_2' \subseteq T$ and (P1)–(P3) hold.

- **Inductive step ($h \to h-1$, eliminating $d = d_h$):** If $T = T(h-1,\dots)$ is tautological, derive it from an axiom. Otherwise $T = G \uplus d_{t_0}^{\mathrm{ext}}$ is non-tautological.
  - Suppose $d \in B_2$ (the case $d \in B_1$ is symmetric). Note $d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{ext}} = d_{t_0}^{\mathrm{ext}} \uplus (d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t_0))$. Let $H := d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}} \setminus \chi_v(t_0) = \ell_1 \lor \dots \lor \ell_q$ (in post-order). If $q = 0$, $T$ equals $T(h, A_1^b, A_2^b\cup\{d\})$ directly ($0$ steps). Otherwise, the $h$-level target $L_0$ for $(A_1^b, A_2^b\cup\{d\})$ is $G \uplus d_{t_0}^{\mathrm{ext}} \uplus H$, and for each $j \in \{1,\dots,q\}$, choosing $C_d^{(j)} \in \mathcal C_1(d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}})$ starting at $\neg \ell_j$ gives the $h$-level target $R_j = G \uplus \neg\ell_j \uplus \ell_{j+1} \uplus \dots \uplus \ell_q \uplus (\text{tail of } d \text{ in } \chi_v(t_0))$, where every literal of $d$ in $\chi_v(t_0)$ is already in $C_\alpha \subseteq G$ (because $d \in B_0^{\mathrm{unsat}}$, so $\alpha$ does not satisfy $d$!). Furthermore, $\mathrm{var}(d_{t_0}^{\mathrm{ext}}) \cap \mathrm{Var}_{t_0} = \emptyset$, so $\mathrm{var}(d_{t_0}^{\mathrm{ext}})$ is disjoint from $\mathrm{ResVars}(R_j) \subseteq \mathrm{Var}_{t_0}\setminus\chi_v(t_0)$.
  - Applying **Lemma 2** (with $E = d_{t_0}^{\mathrm{ext}}$) derives $T = G \uplus d_{t_0}^{\mathrm{ext}}$ in at most $q+1$ steps, preserving (P1)–(P3)!

At $h = 0$, we obtain $M(t_0,\alpha,A,\vec C)$ satisfying (P0)–(P3).
Counting steps across all $h=p,\dots,0$ for a fixed $(t_0,\alpha,A,\vec C)$: the number of leaves and internal nodes in the induction tree is at most
$$(p+1) \cdot \prod_{d \in B_2} (1 + |d_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}}\setminus\chi_v(t_0)|) \cdot \prod_{d \in B_1} (1 + |d_{t_2}^{\mathrm{int}}\setminus\chi_v(t_0)|) \le (w+2) \cdot 2^{w+1} \cdot 2^{w+1},$$
each base leaf ($h = p$) performs at most $2 \prod_{c \in E_1} (1 + |c_{t_1}^{\mathrm{int}}\setminus\chi_v(t_0)|) + 1 \le 2^{w+2} + 1$ steps (respectively $2^{w+2} + 1$ for $D_2$ via the same geometric series as Case 3, plus the final weakening step), and each internal node ($h \to h-1$) performs at most $q + 1 \le 2^{w+1}$ steps via Lemma 2. Thus the total number of steps per tuple $(t_0,\alpha,A,\vec C)$ at $t_0$ is at most $(w+2) \cdot 2^{2w+2} \cdot (2^{w+2} + 1) \le (w+2) \cdot 2^{3w+5} = 2^{O(w)}$ by Lemma 1 applied to $t_1$ and $t_2$.

## 5. Total Length Bound and Conclusion

At the root $r$, $(r,\emptyset,\emptyset)$ is inconsistent (since $F$ is unsatisfiable), and $\mathcal E_{\mathrm{plog}}(r,\emptyset,\emptyset) = \{\bot\}$. Thus our bottom-up construction produces an $\mathrm{mRes}$ refutation $\pi$ of $F$.

By Lemma 1, at each node $t \in V(T)$, there are at most $2^{|\chi(t)|} \le 2^{w+1}$ inconsistent states $(t,\alpha,A)$ and at most $2^{O(w\log(w+2))}$ choice tuples $\vec C$ per state. For each $(t,\alpha,A,\vec C)$, we perform $O(1)$ steps at `intro_c` and `forget_v`, at most $k+1$ steps at `forget_c`, and $2^{O(w)}$ steps at `intro_v` and `join`. Multiplying by $|V(T)| = O(k(n+m)w)$ gives
$$|\pi| \le |V(T)| \cdot 2^{O(w\log(w+2))} \cdot \bigl(k + 2^{O(w)}\bigr) = k^2(n+m)\, 2^{O(w\log(w+2))}.$$
By construction, every tautological target and every unused premise $R_j$ of a non-variable-eliminating resolution step (Lemma 2) is derived in $1$ step directly from an axiom $v \uplus \neg v$ with zero incoming resolution edges; hence by Property (P2), every directed path in the proof DAG $G_\pi$ itself resolves each variable in at most one variable-eliminating resolution step (matching the proof of Lemma `lem:mres-to-res`, `main.tex` L4376–4384). Applying Lemma `lem:mres-to-res` of `arXiv:2610.02047v1` converts $\pi$ into a **regular resolution refutation** $\pi'$ of $F$ of length $|\pi'| \le |\pi| \le k^2(n+m)\, 2^{O(w\log(w+2))} \le (n+m)^3\, 2^{O(w\log(w+2))}$, completing the proof of Theorem 1. $\square$
